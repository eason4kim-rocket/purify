# Purify Search — Image Direction V1

## Core idea: Evidence Realism

Purify imagery should feel like the real world has been observed with unusual precision.
Technology is expressed through structure, comparison, convergence, and traceability—not
through science-fiction decoration.

The site uses two coordinated image systems and one non-image graphic grammar:

- **Editorial abstraction / Mineral Evidence** — square, tactile art covers that turn
  Search, Assimilation, and Resolution into a visual sequence.
- **Documentary reality / Evidence Realism** — wide natural and human imagery that shows
  the world from which data and meaning originate.
- **Purify Matrix / Graphic grammar** — flat mineral-blue square modules on white. It is
  reserved for the Hero field and one Living Memory composition; it is not photography,
  not a point cloud, and not a decorative background repeated through the page.

The documentary system has three roles:

1. **Nature / Ground Truth** — the world before it becomes data.
2. **Human / Human Measure** — people observing, comparing, and making responsible judgments.
3. **Evidence / Convergence** — conflicting sources resolving into one dependable fact.

## Homepage allocation

- **Hero:** no photography. Keep the white background and Purify's animated mineral-blue block matrix.
- **Act II / World in Motion:** use `ground-truth-glacial-v1.png` as the static visual anchor and future video opening-frame reference.
- **Act III / Living Memory:** use `memory-field-graphic-v2.svg` as one clear abstract
  composition, followed by `human-measure-researcher-v1.png` as a separate documentary
  scene. The graphic never forms a person, map, logo, or literal object.
- **Living Evidence:** use the three square abstract covers as a sequence—`signal-field-v1.png`, `living-memory-v1.png`, and `resolved-fact-v2.png`—followed by real Search, Assimilation, and Self-healing evidence UI. The wide `evidence-convergence-v1.png` remains an optional chapter opener.
- **Act IV / Future:** return to typography, white space, and an open horizon. Do not add another image wall.

## Asset rules

- Narrative media uses wide **16:9 or 3:2** crops.
- **1:1** imagery is reserved for the three-part product editorial sequence, Journal, and other scannable content indexes.
- Titles and body copy sit outside photography. If an overlay is unavoidable, limit it to one short label or one number.
- Use natural or physically explainable light. Preserve water, skin, paper, glass, stone, and plant texture.
- Do not apply a global blue wash. Mineral blue remains a small semantic accent.
- Use 0–8 px corner radii, no decorative card shadows, and no fake glassmorphism.
- Generated concept images must never be presented as real customers, partners, or documented events.

## Avoid

- OpenAI-style soft-focus gradient covers
- robots, glowing brains, server rooms, code rain, HUDs, holograms
- blue circuit boards, generic data tunnels, and floating UI panels
- plastic skin, staged stock-photo smiles, teal-orange grading, excessive bloom
- repeated square decoration as a page background outside the Hero and one Act III composition; square editorial covers remain self-contained image objects
