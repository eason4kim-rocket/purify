# Final image prompt set

Generation mode: Codex built-in `image_gen` tool. The first frame established
the look; later frames used one or more earlier results as visual references.
All outputs were generated as 16:9 cinematic keyframes with no text, logos, or
watermarks.

## Shared visual specification

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: 16:9 cinematic keyframe for a wordless AI brand film
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial cinema, tactile 35mm texture,
restrained contemporary brand-film art direction
Composition/framing: wide 16:9; meaningful subjects stay in the right half;
keep the left 38–45 percent calm enough for later website copy
Lighting/mood: clear soft daylight, curious, humane, lightly active rather
than solemn or triumphant
Color palette: ivory, mineral blue, ocean teal, graphite, true skin tones
Constraints: no text, numbers, logos, watermark, legible UI, neon, heroic
poses, fantasy glow, generic AI imagery, or environmental-campaign styling
```

## 01 — Whale / nature

```text
A bright waterline view of a real humpback whale lifting its tail above a calm
open ocean, with a very small working marine research boat far in the right
background. Pale morning sky, real sea haze, anatomically plausible tail,
70mm long-lens feeling, fine droplets, salt haze, no dramatic sunset or
exaggerated splash.
```

## 02 — Radio telescope / technology

```text
A real weathered radio telescope dish slowly tilting upward in clear pale
daylight. Its dish arc and position deliberately match the whale-tail arc from
the previous frame. Authentic bolts, seams, maintenance wear, tiny technician
for scale; no futuristic complex, star field, or sci-fi architecture.
```

## 03 — Biosensor / humanity and care

```text
A caregiver's natural hands gently place a small round matte biosensor on the
upper chest of an ordinary adult wearing a soft off-white cotton shirt. Quiet
sunlit room, honest pores and fabric fibers, intimate 85mm close-up, precise
and humane without sentimentality, hospital drama, alarms, needles, or a
futuristic device.
```

## 04 — Hydrophone deck / people observing nature

```text
On the deck of a small working marine research boat, a researcher in practical
salt-worn clothing clips a weighted hydrophone to a wet graphite cable and
releases it toward the sea. Bright open ocean, precise quick action, wet rope,
scratched metal, no lab coat, storm, heroic gaze, or oversized device.
```

## 05 — Hydrophone underwater / signal before data

```text
A compact real hydrophone descends along a thin dark cable through clear open
seawater. Natural particles and refracted daylight; a humpback whale is only a
distant soft silhouette in the far right background. Authentic water optics,
gentle motion, no visible sound waves, glowing cable, bioluminescent fantasy,
or interface overlay.
```

## 06 — Listening / human attention

```text
Inside a small marine research boat cabin, a real researcher in ordinary
practical clothing gently holds one headphone cup to one ear while still
hearing the room, concentrating on a subtle ocean signal. Candid three-quarter
profile, bright natural window light, tiny focus shift, no staged smile,
dramatic revelation, lab coat, futuristic headset, or visible UI.
```

## 10 — Tactile signals / data as material

```text
Directly overhead, two natural human hands place three separate translucent
signal films onto a large ivory light table. One carries an organic ocean-call
spectrogram, one a calm life-signal curve, one sparse radio-astronomy pulse
marks. Imperfect films with fingerprints and real edges overlap without
becoming a perfect answer. No labels, axes, text, keyboard, laptop, hologram,
or geometric UI grid.
```

## 14 — Research collaboration / progress

```text
Two real radio-astronomy researchers walk naturally past a weathered telescope
array carrying one small equipment case. Authentic high-altitude landscape,
light wind, ordinary work clothing, candid purposeful stride. No corporate
team photo, handshake, pointing at the sky, heroic stance, or futuristic base.
```

## 15 — Open ending / nature and people continue

```text
A very wide bright open ocean with the same small working research boat in the
far right third and a humpback whale breathing at a safe distance. A researcher
quietly tends the hydrophone cable. More distant and spacious than the opening,
with pale open sky and calm sea; no sunset, dramatic breach, cheering, or
perfect commercial gloss.
```
